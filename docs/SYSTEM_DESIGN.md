# VayuCell-1000 — complete system design

**Unit:** VayuCell-1000  
**Duty:** 1 000 m³/h hotspot air purification (construction gate, depot doorway, landfill fence, school corridor)  
**Site class:** outdoor / semi-outdoor, Mumbai monsoon, chloride aerosol  
**Not in scope:** city-scale ambient cleaning, home HEPA, DAC/CCUS as the product

This document is the design authority. Numbers are generated by `python -m vayucell.sizing`. Safety sequence is `vayucell.controller`.

---

## 1. What the machine does

Pull a defined air stream through three process stages:

| Stage | Mechanism | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Wet ESP | charged droplets + plates | PM10, PM2.5, ultrafines, sea salt |
| B DBD plasma | OH / O / O₃ from humid air | NO → NO₂, VOC fragments |
| C Mn–Ce honeycomb | catalytic oxidation + O₃ sink | residual VOC, **ozone**, leftover NO₂ |

People breathe the **outlet**. Success is a measured drop at the outlet and 20 m downwind versus upwind, not a skyline render.

Nominal envelope (1000 m³/h, 400 × 400 mm duct):

- Face velocity **1.74 m/s** (WESP-safe 0.8–3.0)
- WESP residence **0.52 s**, plasma **0.29 s**, catalyst **0.17 s**
- Plasma specific energy **7.2 J/L** (ambient gases + catalyst, not industrial flue)
- Electrical budget **~3.8 kW** → **3.8 kWh / 1 000 m³**

## 2. Process flow (P&ID)

```
                    ┌ e-stop / door / leak ┐
AMBIENT ──► [0] insect+rain hood
         ──► [1] coalescing demister
         ──► [2] WESP (spray + HV + collection)
                    │
                    ├ water ↻ sump → pH/level → blowdown
                    │
         ──► [3] DBD plasma (ceramic dielectric)
         ──► [4] Mn–Ce honeycomb + ozone destructor
         ──► [5] outlet cone + ozone/NO₂/PM sensors
         ──► CLEAN AIR
```

**Why this order:** particles first so dielectric surfaces do not foul and arc. Plasma second so NO/VOC are oxidised. Catalyst last so **ozone cannot leave the box**. A wet stage after plasma would also absorb HNO₃; v1 uses WESP liquor as the nitrate sink *and* a slightly alkaline sump so NO₂ that back-mixes or condenses is captured. v2 may add a dedicated packed scrubber after plasma if NOx slip is high.

## 4. Stage A — Wet electrostatic precipitator

**Function:** charge and collect particles down to ~0.1 µm; humid air is an advantage (higher permittivity, droplet nucleii).

**Geometry (v1):**

- 9 vertical 316L / Hastelloy-C276 discharge wires, 0.3 mm, in 3×3, 400 mm field  
- Collection: 8 parallel PP plates, 400 × 900 mm, 45 mm pitch, water-irrigated  
- Spray: 12 hollow-cone PP nozzles, 15 L/min total, 1.5 bar  
- HV: **−40 kV DC**, current limit **8 mA** (~300 W including losses)  
- Polarity: negative corona (higher current, watch ozone; catalyst is the sink)

**Water loop:**

- Sump 80 L, HRT ~5.3 min at 15 L/min  
- Recirculation pump 20 L/min magnetic drive, PVDF  
- pH 6.8–8.5 with 5% NaOH dosing (peristaltic, 1 L/h max)  
- Conductivity / TDS blowdown when conductivity > 4 mS/cm or level > 85%  
- Mesh strainer 1 mm + bag filter 50 µm on spray header  
- **No chlorine generation:** no chloride electrolysis; WESP is electrostatic not electrochemical; anode/cathode of plasma is dielectric-barrier not brine cells

**Collected product:** wet cake + nitrate/sulphate liquor. Bag solids; pH-neutralise liquor; dispose as non-hazardous wastewater only after local lab TCLP-style screen (construction dust may carry metals).

---

## 5. Stage B — Dielectric-barrier discharge plasma

**Function:** in 70–90% RH, electron impact and water produce **OH**, atomic O, and some O₃. These oxidise NO and VOCs. Energy is **not** sized for CO₂ destruction.

**Geometry (v1):**

- 6 parallel-plate cells, each 350 × 400 mm  
- Gap **2.5 mm**, dielectric: 1 mm alumina or quartz on both electrodes  
- Electrodes: aluminium with conformal coating, or stainless mesh on dielectric  
- Air flows **along** the plates (long path), not just through one gap  
- Cooling: process air + cabinet fans; thermal cut-out 70 °C on ferrite

**Power:**

- Topology: series-resonant inverter, **8–15 kHz**, 0–15 kV peak  
- Process power **0–2.0 kW** set by VFD-like current loop (SED 0–7.2 J/L at 1000 m³/h)  
- Isolation transformer, Faraday shield, earth the chassis  
- Start: ramp 5 s after WESP is stable

**Humidity:** do not dry the air. DBD in humid air makes more OH and more surface leakage — keep dielectrics hydrophobic-coated and drain condensate to the sump (not onto HV).

---

## 6. Stage C — Catalyst and ozone destructor

**Function:** finish VOC oxidation; **destroy ozone** so the street is not ozonated.

| Layer | Depth | Media |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 150 mm | MnO₂–CeO₂ / γ-Al₂O₃ honeycomb 200 cpsi |
| 2 | 100 mm | commercial ozone-killer MnO₂ (Carus / equivalent) |
| 3 | 50 mm | optional alkaline-impregnated carbon (NO₂ polish) — skip if fire risk at site |

Cassette slides out like a filter. Face velocity 1.74 m/s; if ΔP > 900 Pa, wash or replace.

**Do not** use PGM (Pt/Pd) on v1 (cost + poisoning by Mumbai chloride).

Regeneration: monthly water rinse of honeycomb if dust breakthrough; dry before HV run.

## 7. Electrical

| Circuit | Voltage | Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mains | 415 V 3φ 50 Hz (or 230 V 1φ for lab 400 m³/h derate) | 32 A MCCB, Type B RCD 30 mA on 230 V aux |
| Fan VFD | 415 V | motor PTC |
| WESP HV | −40 kV DC | current limit, arc detect, dump resistor |
| Plasma | HF HV | door interlock NC, 2-channel |
| 24 V DC | 240 W PSU | PLC, sensors, dosing |
| Equipotential | 16 mm² PE to skid | all doors bonded |

**Interlocks (hardwired, not only software):** e-stop, both HV doors, water leak tray float, RCD. Software in `vayucell.controller` is additional.

Sequence: **OFF → STANDBY → PREPURGE (fan+pump) → WESP HV → PLASMA → RUN**. Fault: plasma and WESP off, fan remains for purge. Outlet O₃ ≥ **80 ppb** trips HV.

## 9. Controls

See `vayucell/controller.py` (executable spec).

PLC class: cheap is fine (ESP32 + safety relays is **not** enough for HV). Use a **safety relay pair** for doors/e-stop and a small PLC (Delta / Siemens Logo / equivalent) for sequence. The Python module is the logic to port, not the field controller.

## 10. Performance targets (v1, not a guarantee)

Chamber, 30 °C, 75% RH, 1000 m³/h, inlet ~150 µg/m³ PM2.5, ~80 ppb NO₂, ~300 ppb toluene-equivalent:

| Species | Outlet target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PM2.5 | ≥ 80% reduction | WESP |
| Ultrafine count | ≥ 70% | WESP |
| NO₂ | ≥ 40% | plasma + wet + polish; if missed, add post-plasma packed bed |
| VOC | ≥ 50% | SED 7 J/L + catalyst |
| O₃ slip | < 50 ppb continuous, trip 80 | catalyst mandatory |

Field (20 m downwind vs 20 m upwind, same height): directional improvement, not 80% — wind dominates. Report both.

## 11. Fabrication sequence (India)

1. PP/FRP duct and sump (local FRP shop, Navi Mumbai / Vasai)  
2. Plate pack + spray manifold (CNC PP)  
3. HV WESP supply (industrial ESP OEM or Spellman-class)  
4. Plasma inverter (power-electronics shop + potting)  
5. Catalyst honeycomb (import MnO₂ block if needed)  
6. Skid, fan, VFD, PLC panel (any CE-minded panel builder)  
7. FAT in a 10 m³ plastic tunnel with incense + NO₂ bottle (lab SOP, gas-certified tech)

**Do not** energise HV without: bonded earth, two-person rule, HV gloves, ozone monitor on.

---

## 12. What not to build

- Smog tower / “clean a ward”  
- Consumer HEPA cube  
- Amine or DAC module as v1  
- Electrolytic seawater / chlorine  
- Uncontrolled plasma (ozone cannon)  
- Bare 304 stainless in the spray zone
