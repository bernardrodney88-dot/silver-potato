# Mumbai & Navi Mumbai startups you can approach with Cursor

This is a working outreach list, not a directory of every funded company in MMR. The filter is simple: **small enough to take a cold meeting**, **clear product pain**, and **a 3–10 day build you can ship with Cursor** (web app, internal tool, WhatsApp/ops bot, dashboard, plugin, landing + CRM, OCR workflow, test suite, or a customer-facing AI feature).

Skip pitching unicorns (Jupiter, Razorpay, PharmEasy, etc.). They already have large engineering teams. Target **1–50 person companies**, **recently funded seed/Series A**, and **incubator tenants** who will sit with you the same week.

---

## How to sell “I use Cursor”

Do not say “I use AI to code.” Founders hear that every day. Say:

> I ship a working prototype in days, not sprints. I will come with a demo of *your* product gap, not a slide deck.

**What you can actually deliver with Cursor (high close rate):**

| Type | Why founders pay | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal ops dashboard | Replaces Excel / WhatsApp chaos | 3–7 days |
| WhatsApp / Telegram ops bot | Sales & delivery already live there | 2–5 days |
| Customer portal / self-serve | Cuts support tickets | 1–2 weeks |
| Document OCR + checklist | GST, labels, KYC, invoices | 4–10 days |
| Chrome / Shopify / Tally plugin | Sits next to a product they already sell | 3–7 days |
| Demo-able AI feature | Helps them close *their* next client | 3–7 days |
| Test suite + bug bash | They are hiring / shipping fast | 3–5 days |

**What not to pitch:** replacing their core ML model, RBI-licensed lending engine, or hardware/firmware. Offer a **layer around** the core product.

**Best close:** build a 1-day demo on public data, record a 90-second Loom, send it with the email. Cold emails without a demo get ignored.

### 1. OneKiraana — highest fit

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What** | Private labelling for kirana stores: source, clean, pack, label grocery under the shop’s own brand. Processing in **Vashi + Goregaon**. |

| **People** | Bhavin Soni (Founder), Umang Savla (Co-founder). LinkedIn both. |
| **Site** | https://onekiraana.com |
| **Pain** | Kiranas order on WhatsApp. SKUs, MRP, next-day delivery, FSSAI packs. Ops will explode after funding. |

**Cursor pitch (pick one):**

1. **Kirana reorder bot (WhatsApp)** — Store sends photo of empty shelf or “5 kg toor dal, MRP 140.” Bot maps to SKU, confirms qty, creates order, shows ETA. Demo with 8 SKUs.

3. **Pack-line dashboard** — Today’s orders by SKU, pouch count, FSSAI batch, who packed what. Phone-friendly for the Vashi unit.

**Why they say yes:** They are operations-heavy, not a 100-person SaaS org. A WhatsApp bot is a sales tool they can show the next kirana.

### 7. CIBA / CIII incubatees (walk-in, many will have no senior engineer)

Use the public CIBA Mumbai list as a door-opener: **GoFloat, Gabha Ed-Tech, TUSK, Pirates Alert, Dimensionless, SkrapNest, Vertroue Healthcare, CRETO, BiZPay, Rozgarkhoj, MaidHub, Doubt Connect, Ghoshak, Agrograde, Frotels**, etc.

**One offer for all of them:**

> I will pick *one* broken user journey on your site/app, rebuild the happy path in 48 hours, and leave you the repo. If you like it, we scope the next two weeks.

**Example upgrades by type:**

- **Marketplace / bookings (Frotels, MaidHub, Rozgarkhoj):** slot booking + WhatsApp confirmation + Razorpay test mode.
- **Edtech (Gabha, Doubt Connect):** doubt inbox → tagged queue → tutor reply templates.
- **Health (Vertroue, TUSK):** patient intake form + reminder SMS log (no medical advice engine).
- **DARPiN Dynamics (AI, CIII + CIBA):** wrap their model in a **clean API + Next.js demo** so they can sell to enterprises.

**Day 1–2 — Build one hero demo**  
Pick OneKiraana *or* LabelBlind. Ship a public demo (Vercel + dummy data). Record a Loom.

**Day 3 — Walk CIBA Vashi + SIES Nerul**  
Ask for 15 minutes with 3 incubatees. Same script: “I already built X; can I map it to your workflow this week?”

**Day 4 — LinkedIn to 10 founders**  
OneKiraana, CREDmitra, Flowace, Sciative, FactWise, LabelBlind, SportVot, OORJAA, Applied Retention, Postbridgetech.

**Day 5–7 — Custom 4-hour spike**  
If anyone replies, spend half a day on *their* screenshots. That is the close.

## Pricing (so you do not freeze)

- **Paid discovery:** ₹15–25k for 3 days, working prototype they keep.  
- **Sprint:** ₹80k–1.5L for 2 weeks (one production-ish slice + handoff).  
- **Incubator special:** first 48 hours free if they intro you to 2 other founders.

Never lead with equity unless they ask.
